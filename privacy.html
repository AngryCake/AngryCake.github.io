Privacy Policy

Albert is a personal application used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account.

  • Google user data is accessed only to perform actions requested by the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • Data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Albert's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
